# Logo

`bornopata.png` is the Bornopata logo: a white ব on a green rounded square, with two leaves. It is 1024 by 1024 pixels with transparent corners.

- Source: supplied by the project owner on 2026-10-21 as a square image on a white background. It was cropped to the rounded square, the white outside the corners was made transparent, and the edge was trimmed by two pixels to drop the light fringe.
- Use: the settings program's sidebar (`apps/settings/assets/logo.png`, 128 pixels), the launcher and input method icon (`apps/settings/linux/icons/`, 16 to 512 pixels), and the website.
- The Sakura blossom stays as the artwork of the Sakura theme and of the website; it is no longer the logo.

Regenerate the sizes with ImageMagick, for example `magick assets/logo/bornopata.png -filter Lanczos -resize 256x256 apps/settings/linux/icons/256x256/apps/bornopata.png`.
