Dictionary (words.bnd): word counts derived from Bengali Wikipedia (CC BY-SA 4.0, contributors listed in the article histories) and the Mozilla Common Voice Bengali sentence corpus (CC0). The file is distributed under CC BY-SA 4.0 (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/). Only words and how often they occur are kept.
Word pair model (lm.bnd): counts of which word follows which, derived from the Mozilla Common Voice Bengali sentence corpus (CC0), whose Wikipedia sentences are CC BY-SA 4.0. The file is distributed under CC BY-SA 4.0. Only words and pair counts are kept, no sentences.
