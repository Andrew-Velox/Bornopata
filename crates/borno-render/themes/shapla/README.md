# Shapla theme art

- `images/lily.svg`, `images/ripple.svg` and `theme.json`: drawn for Bornopata, GPL-3.0-or-later.
- `images/character.png`: the mascot of the vertical layout, a chibi girl sitting on an edge, with a long white-silver braid fading to teal, a water lily on a lily pad in her hair, a long white embroidered kameez, a teal orna and a lily bud in her hand. The transparent original was trimmed to its bounds and scaled to 288 by 348 pixels, which is 96 by 116 points at the theme's `scale` of 3. Her seat line is 73 points from the top of the picture, where the card's top edge goes.
- `images/character-lying.png`: the same girl lying along an edge with her cheek on her folded arms and the lily bud in her hand, her long braid trailing, for the horizontal layout. Trimmed to its bounds and scaled to 450 by 169 pixels, 150 by 56 points; her lowest point sits on the top border of the card.

Both pictures were supplied by the project owner on 2026-10-22, generated from original prompts (no existing character named).
