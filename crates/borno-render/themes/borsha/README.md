# Borsha theme art

- `images/cloud.svg`, `images/drop.svg` and `theme.json`: drawn for Bornopata, GPL-3.0-or-later.
- `images/character.png`: the mascot of the vertical layout, a chibi girl in a yellow raincoat with a grey-blue bob, peeking from behind an edge with a blue umbrella. The original is 1024 by 1536 pixels with the edge on the right; it was cut at the edge, the stray pixels beyond it were dropped, and it was mirrored so she stands to the right of the card. 290 by 540 pixels (97 by 180 points at `scale` 3). The bottom 22% fades out, so the raincoat dissolves instead of ending in a flat cut.
- `images/character-lying.png`: the same girl lying along an edge with her chin on her folded arms and a closed umbrella beside her, for the horizontal layout. Trimmed to its bounds and scaled to 432 by 181 pixels, 144 by 60 points; her lowest point sits on the top border of the card.

Both pictures were supplied by the project owner on 2026-10-22, generated from original prompts (no existing character named).
