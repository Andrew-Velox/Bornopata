# Jonaki theme art

- `images/moon.svg`, `images/firefly.svg` and `theme.json`: drawn for Bornopata, GPL-3.0-or-later.
- `images/character.png`: the mascot of the vertical layout, a chibi girl with long deep-indigo hair, a gold crescent-moon hairpin, a long indigo kameez with gold trim and a star-patterned orna, holding a lantern jar of fireflies and leaning on an edge. The transparent original is 1024 by 1536 pixels with the edge on the right; it was cut at the edge, the stray pixels beyond it were dropped, and it was mirrored so she stands to the right of the card. 323 by 690 pixels, which is 108 by 230 points at `scale` 3.
- `images/character-lying.png`: the same girl lying along an edge with her cheek on her folded arms and the firefly jar beside her, for the horizontal layout. Trimmed to its bounds and scaled to 528 by 164 pixels, 176 by 55 points; her lowest point sits on the top border of the card.

Both pictures were supplied by the project owner on 2026-10-22, generated from original prompts (no existing character named).
