Beside a Bangla candidate you may see a short English meaning in grey, with its part of speech:

```text
1  আমি    pron. I
2  বই     n. book
3  ভালো   adj. good
```

It is there so you pick up English words while you write Bangla. It is always secondary: it never changes the order of the candidates, never delays them, and one gloss is shown per word, with at most two meanings.

## What is covered

The glosses come from English Wiktionary (via the Kaikki.org extract), cleaned up for short display. They cover part of the **5,000 most frequent Bangla words** (about 2,450 words have one so far); other words show nothing. The glosses have not yet been reviewed by a Bangla speaker, so a meaning may occasionally be wrong or oddly chosen. Reports are welcome; see [Feedback](../help/feedback.md).

## Turn it off

```toml
[general]
gloss = false
```

in `~/.config/bornopata/config.toml`. Without the file `gloss-en.bnd` in `~/.local/share/bornopata/` nothing is shown either.
