---
title: Feedback
order: 4
description: How to report a bug, a wrong spelling or a wrong English gloss, and what to include.
---

Bornopata is a preview and your reports shape it. Open an issue on the project's repository.

**For a bug**, include: the Bornopata version (`borno-linux-server --version`), your distribution, desktop or compositor, the application, what you typed, what you expected and what happened. Add the relevant lines of the log (`~/.local/state/bornopata/logs/`); they never contain what you typed.

**For a wrong or missing word**, give the Roman letters you typed and the Bangla word you expected. If it is a spelling people commonly type, it helps tune the ranking.

**For a wrong English gloss**, give the Bangla word and the meaning you think is right.

Contributions are welcome under GPL-3.0-or-later; see the project's contributing guide.
