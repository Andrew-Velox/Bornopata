Bornopata uses the **Avro phonetic** rules: you type the sound in Roman letters. **Capitals matter**: `t` and `T` are different letters, so keep the shift key in mind.

You rarely need to know the exact rule, because the dictionary offers the real word near whatever you type (`dhonnobad` finds `ধন্যবাদ`, `shomoy` finds `সময়`; see [The candidate list](candidates.md)). The tables are for when you want precise control.

## Consonants

| Type | Get | Type | Get | Type | Get |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `k` | ক | `kh` | খ | `g` | গ |
| `gh` | ঘ | `Ng` | ঙ | `c` | চ |
| `ch` | ছ | `j` | জ | `jh` | ঝ |
| `NG` | ঞ | `T` | ট | `Th` | ঠ |
| `D` | ড | `Dh` | ঢ | `N` | ণ |
| `t` | ত | `th` | থ | `d` | দ |
| `dh` | ধ | `n` | ন | `p` | প |
| `ph` or `f` | ফ | `b` | ব | `bh` or `v` | ভ |
| `m` | ম | `z` | য | `r` | র |
| `l` | ল | `sh` or `S` | শ | `Sh` | ষ |
| `s` | স | `h` | হ | `R` | ড় |
| `Rh` | ঢ় | `Y` | য় | `ng` | ং |

`y` between letters is `য়` as well (`koy` gives `কয়`), and `ng` gives `ং` while `Ng` gives `ঙ`.

## Vowels

A vowel at the start of a word is a full vowel letter; after a consonant it is a vowel sign.

| Type | Alone | After `k` or `b` |
|---|---|---|
| `o` | অ | (no sign: `b` gives `ব`) |
| `a` | আ | `কা`, `বা` |
| `i` | ই | `কি`, `বি` |
| `I` | ঈ | `কী`, `বী` |
| `u` | উ | `কু`, `বু` |
| `U` | ঊ | `কূ`, `বূ` |
| `e` | এ | `কে`, `বে` |
| `O` | ও | `কো`, `বো` |
| `OI` | ঐ | `কৈ`, `বৈ` |
| `OU` | ঔ | `কৌ`, `বৌ` |
| `rri` | ঋ | `কৃ` (`krri`) |

## Joined letters (conjuncts)

Two consonants typed one after the other join automatically.

| Type | Get |
|---|---|
| `kr` | ক্র |
| `bri` | ব্রি |
| `khy` | খ্য |
| `tt` | ত্ত |
| `kShm` | ক্ষ্ম |
| `shikkha` | শিক্ষা |
| `biggan` | বিজ্ঞান |

## Marks and punctuation

| Type | Get |
|---|---|
| `^` after a letter | ঁ (chandrabindu): `cha^d` gives `ছাঁদ` |
| `:` after a letter | ঃ (visarga): `ba:` gives `বাঃ` |
| `` ` `` between letters | Keeps them apart: `` r`a `` gives `রআ` instead of `রা` |
| `.` | । (the Bangla full stop) |
| `$` | ৳ (taka sign) |
| digits `0`–`9` | ০–৯ |

`^`, `:` and `` ` `` only count after a letter; at the start of a word they are ordinary punctuation.

## Tips

- `o` is the inherent vowel: it gives `অ` at the start of a word (`onek` gives `অনেক`) and nothing after a consonant (`kotha` gives `কথা`). Type `O` for the sound `ও` or the sign `ো` (`gOru` gives `গোরু`).
- When a word comes out wrong, look down the candidate list before retyping. The dictionary often has the spelling you meant.
- Case is part of the spelling. If `Ta` gives `টা` and you wanted `তা`, you typed a capital `T`.
