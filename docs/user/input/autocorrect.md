Some words cannot be typed the Avro way. `computer` would come out as চম্পুতের, because the Avro rules read the letters, not the English spelling. For about 360 common English words Bornopata knows the Bangla spelling and puts it first.

```text
facebook      1 ফেসবুক   2 ফাচেবুক
computer      1 কম্পিউটার
mobile        1 মোবাইল
```

## What it covers

Internet and phones (`google`, `youtube`, `wifi`, `password`), school (`college`, `university`, `teacher`), health, work and money (`office`, `bank`, `meeting`), travel (`bus`, `train`, `airport`), everyday talk (`hello`, `sorry`, `thanks`), sport, food, things at home, news, the months and some places (`dhaka`, `chittagong`, `london`).

## Capitals

Write the word as you would in English: `facebook`, `Facebook` and `FACEBOOK` all work, and so does `TV`. Any other mix of capitals, such as `FaceBook`, is read as Avro spelling and is left alone.

## Bangla endings

An ending can follow the word, written the Avro way:

| You type | You get |
|---|---|
| `facebooke` | ফেসবুকে |
| `facebooker` | ফেসবুকের |
| `computerke` | কম্পিউটারকে |
| `phonegulo` | ফোনগুলো |
| `tvte` | টিভিতে |

The endings are `e`, `er`, `ke`, `te`, `ra`, `der`, `gulo`, `guli`, `ta`, `ti`, `r`, `o` and `i`. After a word that ends in a vowel (টিভি), `e` and `er` are not added, since Bangla uses `te` and `r` there.

## Words that are left out

A few English words are also everyday Bangla words typed the Avro way: `math` is মাঠ (a field), `call` is চাল (rice) and `cup` is চুপ (quiet). They are not in the list, so they keep giving the Bangla word. If you want one of them as the English word, put it in [your own phrases](phrases.md).

## Choosing something else

The Avro conversion and the dictionary words stay in the list behind the corrected spelling, so any of them is one key away. If you pick another word for the same letters a few times, Bornopata remembers your choice and puts it first, as it does for any word (see [What it learns](../learning/learning.md)). Choosing a corrected spelling is learned like any other word.

## Turning it off

Open Bornopata Settings, Typing, **English words**, or set `autocorrect = false` under `[general]` in the [config file](../settings/config-file.md).
