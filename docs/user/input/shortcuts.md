Three words offer the current date and time, written in Bangla.

| Type | Offers |
|---|---|
| `tarikh` (or `tarik`) | the date as `২০ অক্টোবর ২০২৬`, `২০/১০/২০২৬`, `২০২৬-১০-২০`, and the Bengali calendar date `৫ কার্তিক ১৪৩৩` |
| `somoy` (or `shomoy`) | the time as `২:০৫`, with the part of the day as `দুপুর ২:০৫`, and as `১৪:০৫` |
| `bar` | the weekday, `মঙ্গলবার`, and the weekday with the date, `মঙ্গলবার, ২০ অক্টোবর ২০২৬` |

(The examples are for Tuesday 20 October 2026, 14:05.)

These words are also real Bangla words (`তারিখ` date, `সময়` time, `বার` day), so the real word stays **first** and the date or time follows it. Choose one by its number or move down with `↓`.

## Good to know

- The Bengali calendar is the one of Bangladesh (the 1987 revision): the year starts on 14 April, so 2026-04-14 is 1 Boishakh 1433.
- The part of the day is `ভোর` (4–5), `সকাল` (morning, 6–11), `দুপুর` (noon and early afternoon, 12–14), `বিকেল` (15–16), `সন্ধ্যা` (17–18) or `রাত` (night, from 19 until 3).
- The time and date come from your computer's clock and time zone.
- Dates and times are **never learned**: a date is only right today, so it must not come back first tomorrow.
- To turn these off, set `shortcuts = false` in `[general]`; see [The config file](../settings/config-file.md).
